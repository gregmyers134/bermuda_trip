Night-by-night picks · Rosewood Tucker's Point
Thursday · July 9
Night 1 · Arrival Evening
The Pink Beach Club ›
Beachfront~5 minOpen Air
After a travel day, the last thing you want is a long drive or complicated logistics. The Pink Beach Club is five minutes from the Rosewood, which means you can settle in, freshen up, and be at the table watching the sun go down over the Atlantic without any stress. The open-air setting is immediately transporting — it announces that you're really here — and the kitchen turns out some of the best fresh seafood on the island, with enough variety on the menu that everyone will find something to love.
Friday · July 10
Night 2 · Night on the Town
Barracuda Grill ›
Fine Dining~30 minDiRoNA Award
Barracuda Grill is the restaurant Bermudans themselves reserve for their most important occasions, and it shows. The kitchen has held the DiRoNA award — one of the hemisphere's most prestigious dining honors — for years, and the menu balances exceptional fresh seafood with prime chops, so the table is fully covered. Going tonight gives you a proper night out in Hamilton, Bermuda's capital, before the birthday festivities the following evening. Book the early seating so you can take your time.
Saturday · July 11
Night 3 · Diane's 80th Birthday
🎂 80th
Coconuts at The Reefs ›
Beachfront~35 minFine Dining
This is the one. Coconuts is Bermuda's most celebrated beach dinner — white-linen tables set between pink sand and dramatic cliffs, with the Atlantic stretching to the horizon in every direction. Very few restaurants in Bermuda are actually situated on the beach itself rather than just near the water, and the setting at sunset is genuinely extraordinary. The drive is worth every minute. Call ahead to mention the birthday — the team there is known for making special occasions feel exactly that way.
Sunday · July 12
Night 4 · Final Evening
Island Brasserie ›
SteakhouseOn-propertySeafood
For the last evening, there's something to be said for not going anywhere at all. Island Brasserie is Rosewood's signature restaurant — prime cuts, fresh seafood, and tableside carving in a warm, candlelit setting hung with original murals from Pan American Airways' legendary Sky Club. It's elegant without being a production, and staying on-property means the night ends at your own pace, with a short walk back whenever you're ready. A fitting close to the trip.
